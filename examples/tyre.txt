Process: Change a flat tyre
Start: car on ground, nuts tight, spare in boot, flat on
Goal: spare on, nuts tight, car on ground
1. Jack up the car - needs: car on ground; makes: car raised; ends: car on ground
2. Loosen the nuts - needs: nuts tight, car on ground; makes: nuts loose; ends: nuts tight
3. Remove the flat - needs: nuts loose, car raised; makes: hub free; ends: flat on
4. Fit the spare - needs: hub free, spare in boot; makes: spare on; ends: hub free, spare in boot
5. Lower the car - needs: car raised, spare on; makes: car on ground; ends: car raised
6. Tighten the nuts - needs: nuts loose, car on ground, spare on; makes: nuts tight; ends: nuts loose
